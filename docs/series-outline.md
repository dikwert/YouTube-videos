# HIS GPS KNEW HIS NAME: series outline

Five Shorts, each 55-65 seconds, 1080x1920, 30 fps. All on-screen and spoken text is English.
Parts 2-5 open with a 2-3 second recap card (10 words at most) and end on a cliffhanger.

## The mystery

Ethan Cole, 28, a travel vlogger, rents a jeep on Borneo to film a waterfall. The jeep's GPS knows his name, though he never typed it. The road takes him to a stone temple where travellers' names are carved in the wall, and to a second jeep: identical, same plate, same camera, abandoned in the rain.

The GPS does not navigate by maps. It writes a passenger down before the passenger arrives.

## Rules for the look and the sound

- The plate BRN 2741 and the camera with the ETHAN sticker are the tells. Every jeep in the temple has the same plate.
- The GPS voice is female, calm, polite and flat. It gives a turn just before something bad happens.
- Subtitles: 2-4 words per card, one size, yellow. Red keywords only, 2-4 per part.
- Black is allowed only as the half-second at the end of a part.
- The subtitle block sits at 68% of the frame height. Keep the GPS screen and faces out of that band.

## Part 1: ETHAN NEVER TOLD IT HIS NAME (built, voice pending)

Hook at night in the rain, then "6 HOURS EARLIER". Daylight drive, signal lost, narrow road, the identical abandoned jeep, "WELCOME BACK, ETHAN." Ends with a figure standing in the rain behind him, then half a second of black.

## Part 2: THE OTHER CAMERA

Recap (0-2.5 s): "Last time: his GPS knew his name."

1. 2.5-8 s: Dawn fog. Ethan opens the glovebox of the abandoned jeep. A second camera, mud-caked, with a sticker in his own handwriting: ETHAN.
2. 8-20 s: The card holds one file, dated TOMORROW, 03:12. A man in his jacket walks into the jungle at night and turns to the lens. It is Ethan, soaked. Narrator: "The camera was his. So was the handwriting."
3. 20-33 s: Back in the jeep: "Destination set: Temple of the Listening Stone. 2.4 km." Ethan: "I'm not going near a temple." GPS: "Turn left, Ethan."
4. 33-45 s: Trailhead guesthouse. Uling, who runs it, sees the plate and goes still. "Your jeep came back last night." "I just got here." "That's what he said."
5. 45-58 s: Cliffhanger. On the card, the future Ethan whispers to the lens: "Don't turn left." Behind his voice, the GPS, calm: "Turn left, Ethan."

Keywords: LEFT, TOMORROW.

## Part 3: THE LISTENING STONE

Recap: "Last time: his future self said, don't turn left." (9 words)

1. 2.5-12 s: He turns right, against the GPS. Stone steps, moss, a temple with carved faces that seem to follow the lens.
2. 12-25 s: Inside, walls of carved names with dates. MARCUS HALE, 2019. LENA WARD, 2024. Narrator: "Two names. Two trips that never came back."
3. 25-40 s: Uling follows him in. "The stone keeps everyone the road has taken. It keeps the list before the passenger does."
4. 40-52 s: The last name is his: ETHAN COLE. Beside it, freshly cut: TONIGHT.
5. 52-58 s: Cliffhanger. Chisel sounds from behind the wall. A calm woman's voice, not Uling's: "Found you, Ethan."

Keywords: TONIGHT, NAME.

## Part 4: THE SAME JEEP

Recap: "Last time: his name was already carved in stone." (9 words)

1. 2.5-12 s: Ethan pushes a loose stone panel into a hall: a row of identical jeeps, all plate BRN 2741, vines in the wheel wells, engines ticking. Narrator: "Every jeep was the same jeep."
2. 12-25 s: The last jeep's headlights come on. Inside is Lena Ward, thin and mud-caked, with eyes that have seen the same six hours for two years.
3. 25-40 s: Lena: "It doesn't navigate. It writes you down before you get in. It knew your name before you ever rented the car."
4. 40-52 s: The doors lock. The GPS, calm: "Welcome back, Lena." Then: "Welcome back, Ethan."
5. 52-58 s: Cliffhanger. Lena whispers, "Don't look at the screen." He looks anyway. The reflection in the GPS glass is smiling. He isn't.

Keywords: LENA, WELCOME BACK.

## Part 5: TURN LEFT

Recap: "Last time: Lena had been trapped for two years." (9 words)

1. 2.5-14 s: Lena's rule: never answer when it says your name. The GPS says "Ethan." He keeps his teeth shut.
2. 14-30 s: It tries other voices: Lena's, then Uling's, then his mother's: "Ethan, honey, come home." He refuses each one.
3. 30-45 s: He finds the way out. The stone panel is open and the rain is outside. Chapter card: "6 HOURS EARLIER."
4. 45-58 s: The loop restarts on the waterfall road, and the first voice comes back: "In three hundred feet... turn left, Ethan." He says nothing. Then the GPS speaks in his own voice.
5. 58-60 s: Cliffhanger. In his own voice, very quiet: "Turn left." Black. "THE END?"

Keywords: ETHAN, TURN LEFT.
