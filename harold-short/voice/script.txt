This old man
just bankrupted his town...
with a library book.

Meet Harold.
In 1954,
he borrowed one book.
"Fishing for Beginners."
Due in two weeks.

He forgot.
For seventy years.

Last spring,
he found it
in his attic,
holding up
a wobbly table.

So Harold drove
to the library.
Margaret, the librarian,
scanned the book.
The computer
started smoking.

Ten cents a day.
Plus interest.
Since 1954.
The total?
One point two
million dollars.

Harold had
forty-three dollars...
and a goat.

The judge said,
"Pay with anything
you own."
And Harold remembered.
Grandpa left him
some land.
The land under
the entire town.

Overnight,
the library
owned everything.
The bank.
The bakery.
The fire station.

Margaret became mayor.
Her first law:
whispering only.
Everywhere.

Car horns? Banned.
Loud sneeze?
Ten-dollar fine.

And Harold?
He finally read the book.
Went fishing.
And caught...
another library book.

Overdue since 1931.
Borrowed by...
Margaret's grandfather.
