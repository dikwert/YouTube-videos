Walter showed up
exactly on time.

Ten seconds later,
he was arrested.

This is Tardyville.

Population: two thousand.

And one very
strange law.

Back in 1887,
the town's only clock
ran fifteen minutes slow.

Nobody fixed it.

So the town
made it law.

Everyone must be
fifteen minutes late.
For everything.

The bakery sign says
"Open at seven."
It opens
at seven fifteen.

The school bell rings,
and the kids
wait outside.

Weddings start late.
Buses leave late.
Even birthday cakes
get cut late.

Luckily, nobody here
has ever missed a train.
Unluckily,
that's because
the trains are late too.

Then Walter moved in.
His whole life,
Walter was late.
To everything.

Tardyville was
his dream town.

But on his
very first day,
he was so nervous…
he arrived
at nine o'clock.
Sharp.

The whole office froze.
Someone dropped
their coffee.
Sirens.

The judge set
his trial
for ten.

Walter arrived
at ten fifteen.
Perfectly late.

The judge smiled.
"Not guilty."

Sounds made up?
In German universities,
a class at ten
"c.t." really starts
at ten fifteen.

It's called
the academic quarter.

Walter would
fit right in.
